# Measurement and Decision Gate

Review results weekly through `/admin` and export the underlying datasets before analysis. Apply
the thresholds in `assumption-register.md`; count unique visitors rather than raw repeat events
where the question concerns people.

Proceed to the next product phase only if named-coach search demand and private-review supply are
both supported. Strong landing conversion alone is insufficient. Legal review of publication and
moderation, verification willingness, long-tail coach coverage, and public-source terms remain
separate gates before building a public marketplace or AI-summary pipeline.
