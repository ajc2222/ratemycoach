## Assumptions deliberately left untested

A11–A14 are out of scope by design. Three of them (A12, A13, A14) are **existential** and
are _not_ demand questions — they are legal, operational and trust questions that a landing
page cannot answer. Strong demand results therefore do **not** authorise building the full
marketplace on their own; see the decision gate in `docs/08-measurement.md`.
