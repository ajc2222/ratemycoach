# Post-validation Roadmap

If the decision gate passes:

1. Complete legal review, moderation policy, appeals, and evidence-retention procedures.
2. Replace process-local rate limiting with a shared store and add operator audit logging.
3. Pilot review verification without collecting unnecessary identity documents.
4. Research and onboard real coaches with correction and claim workflows.
5. Build public review publication only after moderation operations are proven.
6. Evaluate public-source summaries separately against platform terms and source attribution needs.

Authentication, payments, recommendations, messaging, and promoted placement remain outside the
smoke test and should be prioritized only from validated demand.
