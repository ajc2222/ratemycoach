# Content Safety

- All directory entries are fictional and visibly marked as demonstrations.
- The product displays no fabricated ratings, review counts, testimonials, scarcity, or outcomes.
- Reviews remain private and require moderation before any future publication.
- Separate consent controls publication and follow-up contact.
- Server-side screening rejects criminal allegations and exposed contact details with neutral
  rewriting guidance. Lower-risk flags remain attached for operator review.
- Natural/enhanced labels describe coaching focus; they are not claims about an athlete.
- User text is limited, stripped of control characters and tag-shaped markup, and never rendered
  as HTML.

Automated screening is a triage layer, not a substitute for legal review or human moderation.
